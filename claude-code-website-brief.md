# AKAR Website Brief for Claude Code

## Project Goal
Create a premium corporate website for **AKAR GmbH**, a full-service partner for Turkish and international food distribution in Europe. The site should present AKAR as an established, large-scale FMCG distribution company with over 30 years of experience, broad logistics capacity, strong product breadth, and a clear B2B focus. The website should feel modern, high-trust, product-rich, and motion-led without becoming playful or childish.[file:1][web:3][web:4]

## Reference Direction
Use **only** [bombon.rs](https://bombon.rs/) as the visual and interaction reference. Ignore all other references mentioned in the source brief. The goal is **not** to copy Bombon literally, but to translate its strongest qualities into a more corporate food distribution context: elegant motion, high-end presentation, immersive section transitions, polished mobile behavior, and a refined premium feel.[file:1][web:3]

## What to Borrow from Bombon
Translate these traits from Bombon into the new site:

- Strong first-screen visual storytelling with large, immersive hero composition.[web:3]
- Premium motion and transition design that makes the site feel crafted rather than templated.[web:3]
- Clean, modern layouts with confident spacing and high visual control.[web:3]
- Editorial-feeling product presentation instead of generic B2B blocks.[web:2][web:3]
- Seamless desktop and mobile quality, with interactions that feel intentional on both.[web:3]

## What the New Site Should Feel Like
The website should feel:

- Premium
- Established
- International
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- Operationally strong
- Visually rich but still business-focused

This is a **B2B FMCG distribution website**, not a DTC candy shop. So keep the cinematic polish and motion mindset of Bombon, but adapt the tone toward scale, reliability, logistics strength, and commercial professionalism.[file:1][web:2][web:3]

## Brand Positioning
Core positioning from the brief:

**Mit unserer Leidenschaft für das Beste.**  
**Ihr Full-Service-Partner für türkische und internationale Lebensmittel und Distribution in Europa.**

AKAR should be positioned as a leading international distribution and solutions partner in the FMCG sector, serving retail with Turkish and international products across Europe. The company combines product sourcing, sales, logistics, customs/export capability, quality assurance, and digital enablement under one roof.[file:1]

## Key Facts to Surface Prominently
These numbers should appear early on the site as a trust/stat bar or animated metrics section:

- 30+ Jahre Erfahrung
- 14 Vertriebsniederlassungen
- 7 Europäische Länder
- 15.000+ Verkaufsstellen
- 50.500 Palettenkapazität
- 800 aktive Produkte

These metrics are central proof points and should be visualized with premium motion, not as plain static counters.[file:1]

## Primary Audience
Design primarily for:

- Retail buyers
- Trade partners
- B2B customers
- Brand principals / manufacturers
- Distribution partners
- Potential large retail accounts

The site should communicate scale and executional strength quickly to decision-makers.[file:1]

## Information Architecture
Build the site as a polished corporate marketing website with the following main sections.

### 1. Hero
Hero should immediately communicate:

- AKAR as a full-service FMCG distribution partner
- Turkish and international food expertise
- European distribution footprint
- Premium, high-trust presence

Suggested hero content direction based on the brief:

- Strong headline from the brief
- Supporting copy about 30+ years, FMCG expertise, and network across Europe
- Large visual composition inspired by Bombon’s immersive style
- Motion-led presentation with floating/hovering product or packaging elements adapted for AKAR’s product world
- CTA toward products / assortment and possibly contact or B2B shop

Use motion intelligently: polished, smooth, and premium. Avoid gimmicks.[file:1][web:3]

### 2. Trust Metrics
Create a visual trust bar or animated KPI strip directly below the hero. This should showcase the six main business facts from the brief in a clear, premium way.[file:1]

### 3. Product Categories
Feature the product range in a visually rich section. The brief lists these product groups:

- Süßes
- Salzgebäck
- Grundnahrungsmittel
- Hülsenfrüchte
- Brotaufstriche
- Fertiggerichte
- Suppen
- Speiseöle
- Saucen & Gewürze
- Eingelegtes
- Getränke
- Tee & Kaffee
- Backwaren
- Haushaltswaren

This section should feel elevated and editorial, not like a basic icon grid. Consider layered cards, horizontal motion, reveal sequences, or premium category tiles inspired by Bombon’s product showcase energy.[file:1][web:2][web:3]

### 4. Assortment / Product Portfolio
Use the quote and positioning from the brief to present the assortment strategy:

> Unser Sortiment vereint über 800+ internationale Produkte mit gezielter Nachfrage im Handel. Von Süßwaren, salzigen Snacks und Getränken bis hin zu ethnischen Spezialitäten und ausgewählten Non-Food-Artikeln bieten wir ein breit aufgestelltes Portfolio, das exakt auf die Anforderungen moderner Handelsformate abgestimmt ist.

Also include the point that the portfolio combines established brands, growth-oriented international trend products, and high-quality private label options. This section should support a CTA such as **„Jetzt Sortiment entdecken“** and leave room for a **B2B shop button**.[file:1]

### 5. Brands
Showcase selected key brands from the brief, such as:

- Ülker
- Almarai
- Bizim Mutfak
- Cola Turka
- Godiva
- Komili
- McVities
- RedBull
- Sera
- Superfresh
- Tat
- Zakia

There are many brands in the brief, so the design should avoid clutter. Use a premium animated marquee, horizontal reveal, or grouped logo wall with elegant motion. Keep it clean and selective if needed.[file:1]

### 6. Company Story
Create an “About / heritage” section around this message:

**Seit 1992 bringen wir ein Stück Heimat in den Handel**

Support it with copy about AKAR as an international distribution and solutions partner near Munich, with more than 30 years of experience, a Europe-wide sales network, and a passion for quality. This section should feel warm, established, and grounded.[file:1]

### 7. European Footprint / Distribution Network
Create a section highlighting the operational network:

- 14 sales/distribution locations
- 7 countries in Europe
- 50,500+ pallet capacity
- Delivery and availability strength
- Presence in Germany, France, Belgium, the Netherlands, Austria, Switzerland, and Sweden

This should feel strategic and infrastructural. Consider a map or high-end geographic visualization, but styled with the elegance and motion discipline inspired by Bombon.[file:1]

### 8. Timeline
Include a visual timeline with the milestones from the brief:

- 1992: Foundation with 100 m² warehouse and one truck
- 1996: First Turkish brand listed in national retail
- 1996–2000: Development of distribution sub-locations
- 2000: Move into 5,000 m² warehouse
- 2001–2002: Development of European sub-distributors
- 2019: New central warehouse in Langenbach near Munich with 24,000 pallets
- 2023: Expansion of regional branches
- 2024: New Hamburg warehouse with 4,000 pallets
- 2025: AKAR Belgium founded and Benelux expansion
- 2026: Switzerland location founded

This section should not look corporate-boring. Use motion, reveal choreography, or immersive storytelling while staying readable.[file:1]

### 9. Capabilities / Services
Feature AKAR’s end-to-end capabilities as a core section. The brief lists:

- Einkauf
- Sales
- Logistik
- Zoll & Export
- Qualität
- Digitalisierung

Each capability should be framed as part of AKAR’s full-service model. This is a good place for a more structured layout, but still elevated visually.[file:1]

### 10. CTA / Contact
End with a strong B2B-oriented CTA area such as:

- Sortiment entdecken
- B2B Shop
- Kontakt aufnehmen
- Vertrieb anfragen

The footer/contact area should feel polished and premium, in line with the overall motion-heavy design language.[file:1][web:4]

## Content Priorities
Prioritize the following messages throughout the site:

1. AKAR has scale.
2. AKAR has heritage.
3. AKAR has a wide and relevant assortment.
4. AKAR operates internationally.
5. AKAR is a full-service partner, not just a wholesaler.
6. AKAR combines operational strength with modern execution.[file:1]

## Visual Direction
Translate Bombon’s design language into a corporate FMCG distribution setting with these principles:

- Immersive, premium hero treatment
- Strong product visuals and packaging presence
- Smooth section transitions and motion separators
- Refined typography with a premium editorial touch
- Rich imagery, but controlled layout discipline
- Mobile-first polish
- Confident use of whitespace and composition
- Premium animation, especially for metrics, categories, timeline, and brand presentation

Avoid flat generic B2B layouts, generic icon circles, and stock-template feature sections. The site should feel custom and brand-led.[web:3][file:1]

## Motion Direction
Motion is important, because the brief explicitly points to animation ideas and Bombon is known for polished animated execution. Use motion in these areas:

- Hero entrance and product movement
- Section separators / transitions
- Floating product or packaging elements
- Animated stats
- Brand/logo motion
- Scroll-based reveals
- Timeline storytelling

Motion should feel premium and smooth, not excessive. It must support trust and craft.[file:1][web:3]

## Tone of Copy
Copy should sound:

- Confident
- Established
- International
- Professional
- Commercially strong
- Clear and not overly corporate-jargon-heavy

It should balance warmth (“ein Stück Heimat”) with operational authority and European scale.[file:1]

## Design Constraints
- Use **bombon.rs as the only reference website**.[file:1][web:2]
- Ignore Big Tree Farms, Mowi, and any other references in the original brief.[file:1]
- Do not build a candy-style consumer shop aesthetic.
- Do not make the site playful in a childish sense.
- Do not use generic SaaS-looking sections.
- Do not reduce the brand to logistics-only; the product world and assortment richness matter too.[file:1][web:3]

## Suggested Page Structure
A strong homepage flow could be:

1. Hero
2. Trust metrics
3. Product categories
4. Assortment proposition + CTA
5. Brand showcase
6. Company story
7. Europe footprint / map
8. Timeline
9. Capabilities
10. Final CTA / contact

This structure follows the content hierarchy in the client brief while using Bombon-inspired pacing and presentation.[file:1][web:3]

## Build Notes for Claude Code
- Create a premium, motion-rich informational website.
- Keep the experience highly visual and modern.
- Design desktop and mobile with equal care.
- Use polished transitions and reveal choreography.
- Emphasize product world, scale, and European distribution strength.
- Treat statistics, categories, and timeline as key storytelling devices.
- Keep everything consistent with the elevated interaction quality seen on Bombon.[web:3][file:1]
